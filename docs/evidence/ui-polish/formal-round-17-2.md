**Necessary fix:** improve long custom-shortcut labels. Use a readable supplied title or graceful truncation, and avoid splitting a hostname inside a word. This is a minor polish fix, not a blocker.

**Optional improvements:**

- Shorten repeated assistant allowance explanations and eliminate the clipped starter-text sliver behind the model menu.
- Break lengthy import instructions into shorter, action-led steps.
- Give dark translation fields slightly stronger boundaries and make the automatic-translation label easier to scan.
- Offer a quicker route to to-dos on narrow new tabs.
- Synchronize the source-picker foreground and backdrop appearance.
- Compress repetitive release disclosures and align the efficiency article’s source-link styling with its other links.

The serif editorial pages, restrained palettes and intentionally fixed-light efficiency article are reasonable design choices. I would preserve those directions.
