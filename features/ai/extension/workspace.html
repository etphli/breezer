    Importing copies your data once. Changes you make in Chrome later stay in Chrome.

    Bookmarks and history

    Opens the import dialog in a new tab. Choose Google Chrome, then pick what to copy.

    Passwords

    1. In Chrome, open Password Manager, then Settings, and choose Export passwords.
    2. Back in Breezer, choose Import passwords and select the exported file.
    3. Delete the exported file afterwards, including from the Trash.

    Connect Organato?

    Organato puts your upcoming exams, assignments and to-dos on new tabs, and lets Breezer AI update them when you ask. It’s optional.

    Create a key in Organato Settings. It’s stored in macOS Keychain, never in this page.

    Turns Organato off on new tabs and in Breezer AI. Continue leaves it for later. You can change either in Settings.
