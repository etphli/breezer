New in Breezer 1.3

A new mark, and Breezer pages at breezer://

The Slipstream logo is two white strokes of wind on ultramarine. It now appears on the app, the tab strip and this page.

Breezer's own pages have their own addresses. Type breezer://settings to open Settings. The address-bar star stays put, so bookmarking works as before.

Tabs that match the window around them

The toolbar and sidebar now share one color in Breezer's own themes. Horizontal tabs drop the New group button that was always on screen.

Folders can take a color too. Turn it on under Settings, then Layout, and choose whether the color fills the folder icon or the whole row. Right-click a folder to pick a preset or type a hex value.

Folder colors start Off and stay on this device. Third-party Chrome themes keep their own palettes.

Ask the assistant about your browser

Ask in your own words how many tabs are open or how much memory Breezer is using. The assistant reads the numbers from the browser instead of guessing.

Answers can include Markdown tables. Each prompt you send has a copy button, the two overlapping squares, so you can reuse it.

Private windows are left out of these counts.

Describe a look, or paste a picture

Tell the assistant what you want, or paste an image, and it proposes a theme: browser colors plus New Tab artwork and animation.

Nothing changes until you choose Apply theme. If you don't like it, Restore previous look puts your earlier colors and New Tab back.

Shortcut icons, with or without Organato

New Tab shortcuts show each site's icon whether Organato is on or off.

Breezer keeps up to eight of those icons for 15 minutes, ready for the next New Tab. Quitting clears them.

Update 1.3.1

Sidebar and New Tab fixes

Animated backgrounds open on a saved still

  1. Opens at once. A still of the opening frame shows first.

  2. Comes alive. The live artwork takes over once it loads.

  3. Rests while hidden. It unloads when you leave the tab and restarts when you return.

Ask about tabs and memory

Breezer AI answers with measured counts, and lists its own memory apart from system RAM.

Theme requests work in plain words too. Ask for dark purple, then apply the preview when you're ready.

Replies keep running when you look away

Close the sidebar or switch tabs or windows. Read-only replies carry on; reopen the sidebar in the same window to read them.

Browser control stops when you leave the sidebar, tab, or window.

Close the model picker with ×

It works while Reasoning and speed is open, and focus returns to the model selector.

Breezer Settings in the address bar

Settings pages show a plain name. Focus the address bar to reveal the full address.

Folder colors are in the folder menu

Right-click or two-finger click a sidebar folder to pick a swatch or enter a custom hex color. If folder colors are Off, picking a color turns them on.

Use Settings, then Layout, to tint the whole row or hide saved colors again.

More room for your New Tab background

The Organato card leaves space on both sides, so your background stays visible with the AI sidebar open in fullscreen. The card holds its place while several new tabs load.

Animated New Tab backgrounds

Breezer shows a saved still of the opening frame while the live artwork loads. The animation unloads when you leave the tab and restarts when you return.

Breezer AI

Ask Breezer AI to make your browser dark purple, then apply the theme preview when you're ready. Ask about tabs or memory to see measured tab counts for this window and all regular windows, with Breezer's memory shown separately from system RAM.

Read-only replies keep running if you close the sidebar or switch tabs or windows. Reopen the sidebar in the original window to read the reply. Browser control stops when you leave the sidebar, tab, or window.

Close the model picker with ×, including while Reasoning and speed is open. Focus returns to the model selector.

Breezer Settings in the address bar

The address bar now shows Breezer Settings while you browse settings. Focus it to reveal the full address.

Update 1.3.6

Search and Ask work better from New Tab

Open an address or keep typing a question

  • New Tab uses the browser's address suggestions and default search engine. Enter opens the selected result or uses your typed address.
  • Press Tab to switch to Ask with your text still in the box. Breezer starts finding available models as soon as you choose Ask.
  • Each new Ask starts a separate chat. Saved conversations can be reopened for up to seven days. Remove individual compact shortcuts with Undo available.

Sidebar polish

The sidebar shows the active site's address and offers access to installed extensions. The top bar follows your saved visibility setting. Space swipes handle momentum better, and a folder's color can tint its whole card. Adjust glass opacity in Experiments.

Clearer maps and images

Maps fit the panel more reliably, including at high latitudes. You can choose to load images cited in replies. With Browser control enabled, the assistant can search a browser tab.

Update 1.3.7

Edit addresses in the sidebar

Search and navigate in place

  • With Address search in the sidebar enabled, type in the sidebar to edit the URL or search. The top bar stays hidden during browsing, and long URLs show their beginning.
  • The floating sidebar has its rounded corners and inset back. The space controls sit directly on the sidebar in a shorter footer.

Settings and the AI model menu

Settings centers the content under its heading. The model picker is wider and has taller rows for each provider's models.

Search, tabs, and chat fixes

New Tab suggestions stay above Organato. Undo restores repeated tabs after Clear Today, and moving a tab to another window keeps its favorite or pin. Breezer AI reports a failed startup and recovers from more browser-action errors.

Known limitations

Google Docs editing

Editor input passed in Chromium test pages. Editing signed-in Google Docs still needs verification.

Touch ID passkeys

Website passkey sign-in with Touch ID still needs Apple-approved signing entitlements that Breezer does not have yet. Saving and filling passwords are separate.

Encrypted sync

Encrypted two-profile and password-sync checks remain pending. Passkeys are excluded from sync.

Enter on Microsoft sign-in

Pressing Enter on Microsoft's password step is still unconfirmed.
