Breezer

A little more your browser

The features introduced in 1.1, plus the latest improvements.

Open Settings

New in this release

Organato on your new tab

New tabs can show your upcoming exams, assignments and to-dos from Organato. To connect, add an Organato API key in Settings, under New tab and Organato. The key is kept in macOS Keychain.

Your own theme color

Choose any color in Settings, under Theme, and Breezer builds a matching theme for the browser and the sidebar.

Password suggestions that stay put

Saved passwords stay suggested while you type in a sign-in form. Nothing is filled until you choose an account.

Updates from Settings

Settings now has a Check for updates button. It looks for stable releases on GitHub. Updating restarts Breezer and keeps your profile and passwords.

Update 1.1.1

Latest improvements

On new tabs

  • Bookmarks appear on new and blank tabs by default. Settings also offers Always and Hidden.
  • Organato exams and assignments share one Upcoming list, with no duplicate items. To-dos stay separate.
  • Remove a frequently visited shortcut with its Remove button or right-click menu. Undo and Restore shortcuts bring it back. Links open immediately, and your history and bookmarks stay intact.

Getting started in Settings

  • A getting-started card explains importing Chrome bookmarks, history and passwords, with clearer Settings controls.

When an AI reply fails

  • The AI sidebar explains failures and offers Edit and resend. It restores your draft without sending automatically or turning browser control back on.

Update 1.1.2

Previews from favourites

Peek from a favourite

  • Peek opens new foreground tabs and links to another site from a favourite in a centred live-page preview over a tinted background. Click outside to close it, or use the expand icon to keep the page as a normal tab. Your saved favourite keeps its original identity.

Favourite tiles

  • Favourite tiles have clearer active and sleeping states, balanced proportions and a compact layout for a narrow sidebar.

Site shortcuts

  • Commonly visited sites use equal-width slots. Long names shorten to fit, so the icons stay evenly spaced. Hover over a shortcut to read its full name.

Update 1.1.3

Keep your favourites at home

  • Peek also opens different pages on the same site, including document and subject links, and supports user-triggered script navigation and initially blank new-tab windows. Form submissions and same-document app routes keep their normal behavior.
  • A short fade introduces Peek without moving its click targets. Reduce Motion skips the animation.
  • Sidebar menus use a tighter dark shadow instead of a pale halo. Failed tab icons show a letter fallback and recover when a valid icon arrives.
  • The sidebar info button beside Settings opens this updates page.

Not yet: Touch ID passkeys

Website passkey sign-in with Touch ID still needs Apple-approved signing entitlements that Breezer does not have yet. This is separate from saving and filling passwords.
