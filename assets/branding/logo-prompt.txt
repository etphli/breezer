Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: final standalone logo and macOS app icon for Breezer, a personal web browser based on Helium.
Primary request: Create a beautiful, distinctive, professionally art-directed Breezer icon. The name suggests air, effortless movement and a light browser. Design one elegant lowercase b monogram sculpted from a single broad flowing ribbon. The ascending stroke curves fluidly into an open, generous rounded counter, with a short aerodynamic taper suggesting a breeze. The silhouette must be intentional and instantly readable at a small size.
Style/medium: immaculate contemporary software identity, restrained sculptural depth, smooth satin surfaces, precise curves, subtle internal lighting. Strong graphic silhouette before any material effects. Airy cool tones appropriate to the name, a luminous mint/aqua ribbon with a little icy blue along the turning edge, on an inky deep teal rounded-square app tile. Avoid a rainbow or a generic neon glow.
Composition/framing: one icon only, square canvas, centered with generous even transparent space outside the rounded-square tile. Tile occupies roughly 82 percent of canvas width. The ribbon mark fills roughly 65 percent of the tile. Frontal orthographic view, no perspective tilt. Rounded-square tile corners are smooth and substantial, with quiet beveling. The deep teal background is confined inside the tile. Actual alpha transparency outside it.
Text: none. The b is the abstract mark itself, not typeset text.
Constraints: clean finished production artwork, elegant negative space, no wordmark, no tagline, no typography, no watermark, no mockup, no surrounding interface, no additional symbols, no decorative particles. Original identity. Do not resemble Chrome's segmented circle, Safari's compass, Firefox's fox, or Edge's curling wave. Avoid ornamental complexity, thin hairlines, exaggerated gloss, excessive reflections, lens flares, thick cast shadows, and visual noise.
