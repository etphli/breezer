Your day opens with every tab

  1. The sites you use most

    Shortcuts sit under the date in one even row. Add your own or hide one, right on the page.

  2. What's coming up

    Connect Organato and new tabs list upcoming exams, assignments and to-dos. Your API key stays in macOS Keychain.

  3. In any colour you like

    Pick a colour in Settings and Breezer builds a matching light or dark theme for the browser and the sidebar.

Tabs that stay out of the way

Tabs live in a sidebar on the left. Pin favourites to the top, group work into spaces and folders, and clear today's tabs with one click when you're done.

  • Peek opens a favourite in a preview without leaving your place.
  • Expanded, compact or auto-hide, at the width you choose.
  • Prefer tabs on top? That's one switch in Settings.

An assistant beside the page, not in the way

Open it on the right whenever you need a hand. It sees the page and a screenshot, and answers with the Codex or Claude account you already use.

Help that you can watch

Turn on browser control and Breezer can click and type for you. A blue wave frames the tab the whole time. Press Escape or Stop to take back over.

Move in from Chrome

Bring over bookmarks, history and passwords from Settings. Breezer is Chromium underneath, so your sites work the way they always have.

Updates you can read

Install updates from Settings. Each release opens an illustrated page once, explaining what changed and what it can't do yet.
